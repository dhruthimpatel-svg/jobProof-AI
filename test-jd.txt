AI/ML Engineer — Growth Analytics

At Northwind Labs, we build analytics products that help e-commerce companies understand shopper behavior. Our platform processes millions of events per day across 40+ retail brands.

About the role

As an AI/ML Engineer on the Growth Analytics team, you will design and ship machine learning models that power recommendation and churn prediction products used by our customers every day.

What you'll be doing

- Design, build, and deploy machine learning models for recommendation systems and churn prediction
- Build and maintain data pipelines that move events from production systems into the modeling environment
- Work with product managers and engineers to translate business questions into ML problems
- Run experiments and A/B tests to measure model impact on retention and revenue
- Monitor model performance in production and retrain models as data drifts
- Mentor junior engineers through code reviews and pairing

What we're looking for

- 2+ years of professional experience shipping machine learning models
- Strong Python skills; you write clean, testable code
- Solid SQL and experience with large datasets
- Hands-on experience with at least one deep learning framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow)
- Experience deploying models to production (REST APIs, batch scoring, or streaming)
- Experience with Docker and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- A/B testing and experimentation fundamentals
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field

Nice to have

- Experience with Kafka or streaming data systems
- Kubernetes and MLOps tooling
- Airflow for pipeline orchestration
- Recommender systems or NLP experience
- Experience mentoring or leading small projects

Benefits

Competitive salary, equity, and full health coverage. Annual learning budget.
